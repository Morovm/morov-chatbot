SECTION 1: Company Introduction

Company Name: TechVision Solutions
Founded: 2015

Industry: Enterprise Software, Artificial Intelligence, Data Analytics

Brief History:
TechVision Solutions was founded in 2015 with a focus on developing enterprise software solutions.
The founding team consisted of four computer engineering graduates from top Iranian universities.
Initially, the company focused on office automation systems for mid-sized businesses.
In 2019, TechVision launched its AI division and began developing NLP-based products.
By 2023, the company had grown to serve over 120 active enterprise clients across technology, commerce, finance, and healthcare sectors.
Today, TechVision focuses on delivering AI-powered, on-premise knowledge management solutions.

SECTION 2: Services & Products
========================================

Product 1: TechVision Org Chat
Description:
An AI-powered conversational assistant that connects to internal company documents.
Designed for fast Q&A on HR policies, procedures, regulations, and technical knowledge.
Fully deployable on internal networks with no external data transmission.
Key features: RAG support, access level control, and Q&A logging.
Target Audience: Mid-to-large enterprises, software companies, holdings, internal operations units.
Pricing:
- Basic Plan: $560/month (up to 50 users)
- Professional Plan: $1,300/month (up to 200 users + analytics dashboard)
- Enterprise Plan: Custom pricing (unlimited users + full customization)

Product 2: TechVision Knowledge Hub
Description:
A platform for unifying documents, guidelines, procedures, and lessons learned.
Features: Advanced search, smart tagging, content versioning, and document approval workflows.
Supports integration with internal file servers and archival systems.
Target Audience: Quality assurance, knowledge management, HR, training, and operations teams.
Pricing:
- Initial Setup: Starting from $3,600
- Annual Maintenance: 20% of contract value
- Custom Development: $96/hour

Product 3: Conversation Analytics Dashboard
Description:
Generates management reports from user interactions with the organizational assistant.
Tracks metrics such as frequent topics, successful response rate, unanswered questions, and response time.
Helps managers identify gaps in documentation and improve knowledge base quality.
Target Audience: HR managers, IT managers, operations managers, knowledge management teams.
Pricing:
- Basic Dashboard: $240/month
- Advanced Analytics: $480/month

Product 4: Deployment, Training & Model Optimization Services
Description:
Full on-premise deployment services including installation, security configuration, knowledge transfer, and end-user training.
Also offers data optimization and prompt improvement services for higher accuracy.
Target Audience: Organizations with limited in-house technical teams or those requiring rapid project execution.
Pricing:
- Quick Deployment Package: $1,900
- Full Deployment + Training: $3,200
- 3-Month Optimization Package: $4,200

SECTION 5: Policies & Guidelines
========================================

Refund Policy:
A 7-day trial period is available for select subscription plans.
Written refund requests submitted within the first 7 days, without extensive operational usage, may be eligible.
Deployment, customization, and on-site training fees are non-refundable.
For enterprise contracts, refund terms follow the specific agreement.

Data Privacy:
All client data belongs to the client. TechVision claims no ownership over content.
Organizational data is never shared with third parties without written client consent.
In on-premise deployments, data is processed and stored exclusively on client infrastructure.
Security incidents are reported to the designated client representative as soon as possible.

Data Retention:
System logs are retained for 90 days by default.
Retention periods can be adjusted with written client approval.
Upon contract termination, data is securely deleted or returned per agreed procedures.

Service Level Agreement (SLA):
Monthly Service Availability:
- Basic Plan: 98.5%
- Professional Plan: 99.2%
- Enterprise Plan: 99.5%
Scheduled maintenance with prior notice is excluded from SLA calculations.

SECTION 6: Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)
========================================

Q1: Does the Org Chat work without internet?
A: Yes. In on-premise deployment, the system operates fully within the internal network.

Q2: What file types are supported for the knowledge base?
A: Text files, PDF, DOCX, internal HTML pages, and structured outputs like CSV are supported.

Q3: Are chatbot answers traceable to their source?
A: Yes. Source sections can be displayed for each answer in Professional and Enterprise plans.

Q4: What is the minimum project setup time?
A: 5 to 10 business days for the Basic plan. Enterprise timelines vary based on data volume and integrations.

Q5: Can user access levels be defined?
A: Yes. Access can be set by organizational unit, job role, or user group.

Q6: What should I do if answers are incorrect?
A: Review the quality and structure of source documents first. TechVision also offers data optimization and retrieval tuning services.

Q7: Is Active Directory integration possible?
A: Yes. For enterprise clients, AD/LDAP authentication integration is available.

Q8: How are backups handled?
A: Backup policy is configured per client infrastructure. Daily backups of the database and system settings are typically recommended.

Q9: Is end-user training provided?
A: Yes. Training packages include an introductory session, user guide, and short workshop for internal administrators.

Q10: What is the process for requesting custom features?
A: Submit a request, receive a needs analysis, and get a formal time and cost estimate. Development proceeds under a supplementary contract.

Q11: Is client data used to train public models?
A: No. Client data is never used for public model training or external sharing without written consent.

Q12: Can the system support multiple languages?
A: Yes. The core system supports multiple languages, though the primary focus remains on high-quality English responses.
